# Outcome counts of the first runs

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `outcome_counts.csv` | outcome counts of the first runs (m2, m3, m4) per kind of limit and kind of cell (own condition or transfer) |
| `failing_cells_primary.csv` | every not applicable transfer of their primary variants |

Both were written before the KITTI rule and the five splits of the change of dataset and detector: KITTI detections
labelled as run, the change of dataset and detector on one split. The outcome table of
[`../../docs/results.md`](../../docs/results.md#outcomes-over-the-five-splits) supersedes them; they are kept because
version 1.0.0 published them.
