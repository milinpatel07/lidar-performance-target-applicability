## Notes on the paper text

Three statements of the submitted paper do not agree with the recorded files. The files are the record; the notes give the values they hold.

**Note 1 (Sect. 5.2, rain).** The paper gives the share of wrong detections as rising "from 0.34 to 0.42" and states that shares refer to all validation frames. Over all validation frames the shares are 0.335 (clear) and 0.416 (rain), which round to 0.33 and 0.42; on the test part of split 0 they are 0.336 and 0.425. Source: `results/supporting_analyses/wrong_share_and_median_split0.csv`.

**Note 2 (Sect. 5.1, KITTI rule).** The paper states that including the detections excluded by the KITTI rule changed no outcome that held on all five splits. In `results/m6_protocol_seeds_weather/` (protocol `kitti_ignore` with the rule, `as_run` without), 9 transfer cells that held one outcome on all five splits with the rule change without it: far → near, u_e, α = 0.01: ????? with the rule, NNN?? without (splits 0 to 4); KITTI → nuScenes, u_e, α = 0.01: ????? with the rule, ?AAA? without (splits 0 to 4); nuScenes → KITTI, u_e, α = 0.01: AAAAA with the rule, AA??? without (splits 0 to 4); far → near, u_e, α = 0.05: AAAAA with the rule, ????? without (splits 0 to 4); KITTI → nuScenes, u_e, α = 0.05: ????? with the rule, AAAAA without (splits 0 to 4); nuScenes → KITTI, u_e, α = 0.05: AAAAA with the rule, ????? without (splits 0 to 4); KITTI → nuScenes, u_e, α = 0.1: ????? with the rule, AAAAA without (splits 0 to 4); nuScenes → KITTI, u_e, α = 0.1: AAAAA with the rule, ????? without (splits 0 to 4); near → far, u_s, α = 0.05: NNNNN with the rule, ???N? without (splits 0 to 4). The not applicable outcome of nuScenes → KITTI for u_s holds on all five splits at every limit with and without the rule.

**Note 3 (Sect. 5.1, retention).** The paper writes "at most 29 % ... (medians, Fig. 4, right)". The largest retention of a transferred threshold with an applicable outcome at 10⁻² is 28.5 % on a single split; the largest median over the splits is 23.6 % (change of dataset u_e nuScenes KITTI). The statements "at most 29 %" without the parenthesis (Sect. 5.1, answer to RQ1; Sect. 7) agree with the files. Source: `results/supporting_analyses/retention_applicable_cells.csv`.
