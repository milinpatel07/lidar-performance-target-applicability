Outcome explorer

Each cell of the grid is one threshold τ selected in the condition of selection so that the false acceptance proportion (FAP) stays at or below the limit α, then tested on the test part of the condition of use. The Hoeffding-Bentkus interval [L, U] of the FAP at 95 % confidence decides the outcome: applicable if U ≤ α, not applicable if L > α, not determined otherwise, and no threshold selected if no candidate passed in the condition of selection. Each cell shows the five splits 0 to 4.

About these data

  • All values are read from data/outcomes.json, which tools/build_explorer_data.py writes from these result files (each detail row links its file and line):
    • division by frame: results/m6_protocol_seeds_weather/A_kitti_range_protocol.csv (KITTI distance), results/m7_scene_split/m7_scene_split.csv (variant frame: nuScenes distance and weather), results/m6_protocol_seeds_weather/B_shift_seeds_protocol.csv (change of dataset and detector);
    • division by recorded sequence: results/m7_scene_split/m7_scene_split.csv (variants scene_frame_unit, scene_scene_unit), results/m8_kitti_drive_split/A_kitti_distance_drive.csv and B_dataset_drive.csv (hb_unit frame or drive);
    • post-hoc: results/post_hoc/outcomes.csv (method A or B).
  • Target limits are 10⁻², 10⁻³ and 10⁻⁴, fixed before the first run. The exploratory limits 0.05† and 0.1† were fixed at the same time to show which outcomes the data can decide; they are not safety targets.
  • Frames of one recorded sequence (KITTI drive, nuScenes scene) are not independent of each other. The division by recorded sequence with the recorded sequence as unit treats the sequence as the independent unit.
  • Post-hoc entries (Methods A and B) were added after the parameters of the study were fixed and the outcomes were known; they are not part of the predictions fixed in advance. For them, the estimate and [L, U] refer to the transformed sequence loss Y, compared with α (see results/post_hoc). They use the KITTI rule.
  • KITTI rule: wrong KITTI detections inside a DontCare region or matching a van are excluded. Without it, the KITTI rows show the files' as_run rows. The nuScenes rows are the same with or without it.
  • Near distance and clear weather are not triggering conditions; they are the conditions with which the thresholds are compared.
