Applicability of performance targets across triggering conditions

An object detector can meet a performance target, a limit α on the share of wrong detections it passes on, by rejecting detections whose uncertainty exceeds a threshold. A threshold selected on data of one condition carries a statistical guarantee only for data like those. The applicability test uses the same threshold under another condition, for example far distance or rain, and decides at a stated error probability whether the target is applicable, not applicable or not determined there, and how many independent recorded sequences an applicable outcome needs.
