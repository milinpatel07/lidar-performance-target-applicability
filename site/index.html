Applicability of performance targets across triggering conditions

An object detector can pass on only the detections whose uncertainty lies at or below a threshold. A performance target sets a limit on the share of wrong detections among them (per frame, averaged over frames), for example 1 %. A threshold chosen on data of one condition carries its statistical guarantee only for data distributed like those.

The applicability test applies the same threshold, unchanged, under another condition, for example far distance or rain, and states at a given error probability whether the target is applicable there, not applicable, or not determined by the data (or that no threshold could be selected in the condition of selection). It also gives the minimum number of independent units (for example recorded sequences) that an applicable outcome needs.
