Independent units needed

Showing that the false acceptance proportion meets a limit α at error probability δ′ requires at least n ≥ ln δ′ / ln(1 − α) independent units per condition and part (Eq. 5 of the paper, the zero-failure bound), with δ′ = δ for selecting and δ′ = δ/2 for testing a threshold. The curves show its inverse, the lowest limit 1 − δ′1/n that n units without a false acceptance can show to be met. The markers place the recorded units of each condition on these curves. Marker heights are computed from n, not measured: they show the best case, in which no unit holds a false acceptance; any unit with a false acceptance raises the lowest limit that the data can show.
